An official website of the WA State Office of the Attorney General. Here's how you know. 

The .gov means its official. 
WA state government websites often end in .wa.gov. Before sharing sensitive information, make sure you're on a government site.

The https:// ensures that you're connecting to the official website and that any information you provide is encrypted and sent securely.
